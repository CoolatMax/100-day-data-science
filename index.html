🔌 EU Renewable Energy Datasets & Tools

Essential data sources and platforms for European renewable energy data science work:

ENTSO-E Transparency Platform

Real-time and historical data on electricity generation, load, prices, and cross-border flows across Europe.

→ Visit ENTSO-E

Open Power System Data

Free, open-source database of European power system data including generation, load, and prices.

→ Visit OPSD

Renewables.ninja

Solar and wind power output simulations at high spatial and temporal resolution for Europe and worldwide.

→ Visit Renewables.ninja

Copernicus C3S Climate Data Store

Climate and energy-related datasets including temperature, solar radiation, and wind speed from EU satellites.

→ Visit C3S

Eurostat Energy Statistics

Official EU energy statistics including renewable shares, consumption, and capacity by country.

→ Visit Eurostat

Nord Pool Spot Market Data

Nordic electricity market data including day-ahead and intraday prices; excellent for time-series forecasting.

→ Visit Nord Pool

EPEX SPOT

Central European power exchange with Germany, Austria, France, and Poland market data and pricing.

→ Visit EPEX SPOT

Global Energy Monitor

Database of power plants and renewable projects in Europe with detailed specifications and timelines.

→ Visit GEM

PyPI Energy Packages

Key Python libraries: entsoe-py, pvlib, windpowerlib, pyomo, pandapower.

→ Visit PyPI

EU Taxonomy & ESG Data

European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) and SFDR disclosures for energy companies.

→ Visit EC Finance
