Open Dataset & Resource Library

Use these sources throughout the roadmap. Prefer EU-relevant data whenever possible. Always check the dataset licence before publishing derived data.

🌱 Renewable Energy / CleanTech

  • Renewables.ninja — simulated European solar and wind generation.
  • ENTSO-E Transparency Platform — European electricity generation, load and transmission data.
  • EU JRC PVGIS — photovoltaic potential and solar-resource data.
  • EUROCONTROL — aviation-related operational datasets and dashboards.
  • data.europa.eu — EU open-data catalogue.
  • Fraunhofer Energy-Charts — German electricity generation and market visualisations.
  • Open-Meteo — open weather API useful for renewable-energy modelling.

✈️ Aerospace / Telemetry

  • NASA PCoE Dataset Repository — turbofan and predictive-maintenance datasets.
  • NASA Prognostics Data Repository — batteries, bearings, turbofan degradation and other PHM datasets.
  • NASA C-MAPSS — simulated turbofan engine degradation.
  • NASA Open Data — scientific and engineering datasets.
  • OpenSky Network — open European aircraft tracking data.
  • EUROCONTROL — European aviation data and research resources.
  • Zenodo — search for aircraft, structural-health-monitoring and aerospace datasets.
